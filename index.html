Your first year with your new dog

Adopting a dog is a great expression of love and caring. Sometimes, however, it can seem a bit daunting, so here are some tried and true tips for new dog owners.

The First Day

  • Be committed to your adoption
  • Check your dogs vaccinations and the microchip ID verification
  • If you have other pets introduce your new pet slowly
  • Pick a food brand and stick with it. Always have plenty of water

The First Month

  • Schedule your dogs first vet visit
  • Make sure your house is secure and safeguarded from your dog
  • Always keep to a regular exercise schedule with your pet
  • Use regular behavior training times for your pet

The First 6 Months

  • If you travel make sure youve made arrangements for your dogs care
  • If your pet travels with you, always have a ready bag prepared to go
  • Be sure to budget your dogs care into your monthly expenses
  • Be sure to monitor your dogs progress and behavior every month

The First Year

  • Make sure your outside doghouse has plenty of shade, food and water
  • Always make sure that your outdoor enclosure is 100% secure

The Purpose of Adoption

  • Helps Eliminate the overabundance of animals that are Abandonded Neglected or Abused
  • Gives those animals a loving caring home that benefits both the owner and the adopted animal

Why Adopting Matters

  • There are over 70 million neglected animals that live in the United States at any one time
  • Adoption is the singular most important way to control the excess of abandoned animals in our country
  • Adoption also keeps our animal shelters busy sending out more for adoption and receiving more new animals
